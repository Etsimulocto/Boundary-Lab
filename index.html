Measurements sampled every 10 ticks. *Link retention compares consecutive ticks. The largest cluster is a connectivity measurement, not proof of biological or physical emergence.

Experiment setup

Experimental integrity: Every change resets the run. Keep the same seed when comparing one variable. A seed controls pseudo-randomness; it does not guarantee causal inference by itself.

Research question

Which assumptions are necessary for persistent, organized behavior to emerge in a minimal geometric system?

What is *not* being claimed

This is a classical toy model. Its links are not fundamental physical fields; its nodes are not quantum particles. Visual clusters alone are not evidence for a projector, a simulation, or a theory of the universe.

Method

Compare a control run against a changed rule with identical seed and starting node count. Repeat across many seeds. Track cluster growth, link persistence and entropy, then inspect whether the differences survive replication.
